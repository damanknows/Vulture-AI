## 1. Source & Metadata Findings
- **Local cache:** `data/benchmark/temporal/cache/epss_2026-07-01.json` (`date: "2026-07-01"`, `retrieved_utc: "2026-11-26T21:37:33Z"`, `7,927` entries).
- **API semantics (`api.first.org/data/v1/epss?date=2026-07-01`):**
  1. The `date` field represents the **archived daily UTC calendar-date snapshot** generated by FIRST's daily EPSS pipeline (not an intraday timestamp).
  2. FIRST typically calculates and publishes daily EPSS scores around **13:30 UTC**; the API returns `YYYY-MM-DD` with **no intraday timestamp**.
  3. The data is **genuinely archived** historical daily scores from FIRST (retrieved retrospectively on `2026-11-26T21:37:33Z`), not current `2026-11` scores.
  4. **Midnight UTC (`2026-07-01T00:00:00Z`) availability cannot be strictly established** because `date=2026-07-01` likely reflects the `2026-07-01` daily run published ~13.5 hours after midnight UTC.
  5. **Future-outcome leakage check:** All `11` positive KEV additions in TEOB occurred between **`2026-07-07` and `2026-09-21`** (at least **6 days after** `2026-07-01`), and all pre-cutoff KEV entries (`<= 2026-07-01`) were excluded. Therefore, no future KEV outcome leaks into EPSS, and the interpretation of TEOB is unchanged.
